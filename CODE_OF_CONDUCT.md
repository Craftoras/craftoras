# Code of Conduct & Collaboration Protocol

## 1. Purpose & Core Principles

The Craftoras project is dedicated to fostering a professional, respectful, and dignified environment for all contributors, researchers, and developers. We believe that healthy collaboration, technical excellence, and mutual respect are essential to building impactful software.

All contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to adhere to this Code of Conduct across all project spaces, issue trackers, pull requests, and official communications.

---

## 2. Professional Standards & Expected Behavior

Contributors are expected to practice the following professional standards:

* **Mutual Respect & Courtesy**: Treat fellow contributors, maintainers, and community members with dignity, respect, and patience.
* **Constructive Collaboration**: Offer constructive, actionable feedback on pull requests and issues. Critique code and architecture, not individuals.
* **Intellectual Integrity & Attribution**: Always credit original authors, respect open-source licenses, and uphold authentic academic and engineering integrity.
* **Graceful Conflict Resolution**: Accept constructive criticism with grace, take responsibility for errors, and focus on optimal engineering solutions for the project.
* **Professional Communication**: Maintain clear, professional, and courteous language in all discussions, documentation, commit messages, and review threads.

---

## 3. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors violate community standards and will not be tolerated:

* **Harassment & Bullying**: Any form of harassment, personal insults, intimidation, or aggressive conduct.
* **Trolling & Disruption**: Intentional derailment of technical discussions, spamming, bad-faith critiques, or hostile comments.
* **Inappropriate Content**: Posting unprofessional, demeaning, or offensive language, imagery, or commentary.
* **Privacy Violations**: Publishing personal, contact, or private information of any contributor without their explicit consent.
* **Defamation & Malicious Behavior**: Making defamatory or malicious claims against contributors, maintainers, or partnering institutions.

---

## 4. Contributing & Review Protocols

To ensure smooth and professional collaboration, contributors must follow these protocols:

1. **Issue First**: Before initiating major features or architectural refactors, open an issue to discuss design and alignment with project leads.
2. **Respectful Code Reviews**: Reviewers must provide helpful, objective feedback with code suggestions. Authors must address comments courteously.
3. **Clean Commit Standards**: Use clear, descriptive Conventional Commit messages (`feat:`, `fix:`, `docs:`, `refactor:`, `test:`).
4. **Tested Submissions**: Ensure all automated tests pass locally before opening a pull request.

---

## 5. Escalation & Enforcement

Community leaders and maintainers are responsible for maintaining the health of the project and will take appropriate corrective actions:

* **Reporting**: If you experience or witness behavior that violates this code, please contact the maintainers at [darrehanrasool@gmail.com](mailto:darrehanrasool@gmail.com) or reach out privately to project leadership.
* **Confidentiality**: All reports are treated confidentially and reviewed impartially.
* **Corrective Actions**: Violations may result in formal written guidance, removal of comments/contributions, or temporary/permanent suspension from the repository.
